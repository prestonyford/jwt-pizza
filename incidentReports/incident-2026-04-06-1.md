# Incident: 2026-04-06 11-48-00

## Summary
Between the hour of 11:48 and 12:14 MDT on 2026-04-06, 100% of users encountered failures in pizza ordering. The event was triggered by a chaos test at 11:48 MDT. The chaos test caused the pizza order service endpoint to fail. The event was detected by the Grafana alerting system. The team started working on the event by reviewing the logs and attempting to order pizza themselves. This severe incident affected 100% of users for 26 minutes.

## Detection
The team acknowledged the incident at 12:09 MDT on 2026-04-06. A Grafana alert was sent out reporting an abnormal amount of failed pizza ordering requests. The HTTP alert, which fires when there is an abnormal amount of 500 status code responses, was immediately fired. Several minutes later, at 12:09 MDT, another alert was fired, reporting pizza creation failures in the server. The time to response could have been improved if the team acknowledged the first alert instead of ignoring it because it fires occasionally. We should tune the alert system so that that alert does not fire as often, and is therefore taken more seriously when it does fire.

## Impact
For 26 minutes between 11:48 and 12:14 MDT, all pizza orders failed. 100% of users were unable to order pizzas, who received error messages upon attempting to order. 0 support cases were raised.

## Timeline
All times are UTC.
- _5:49_ - Alert was sent to the DevOps team that a pizza order request resulted in a status of 500
- _5:50_ - Alert was sent to the DevOps team that a pizza purchase failed
- _6:09_ - Preston Ford acknowledged the alert and began investigating the issue
- _6:13_ - Preston Ford identified the root cause and ended the chaos test

## Response
Preston Ford was alerted bia mobile push and responded to the incident at 12:13 MDT. He resolved the issue by visiting the link given by the pizza service endpoint to end the chaos test. There was a 25 minute delay between the start of the incident and the response.

## Root cause
The cause of the incident was a chaos test. The chaos test caused all pizza order endpoints to fail and respond with a JSON response which included a link to end the chaos test.

## Resolution
The service was restored by ending the chaos test by using the link given by the endpoint. The incident was deemed over when the developer successfully completed a pizza order after ending the chaos test.

The time to mitigation could have been improved by sending fewer alerts, such that the few alerts that are sent are given more attention.

## Prevention
The incident can be prevented by not triggering unwanted chaos tests.

## Action items
- Reduce alert frequency
